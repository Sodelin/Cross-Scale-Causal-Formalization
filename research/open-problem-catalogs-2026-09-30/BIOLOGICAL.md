## BIO-1 — Sparse quartet access with an honest statistical bridge

**Source question.** Dai and Molloy explicitly suggest reducing sampled four-taxon subsets in NANUQ+, or replacing collections with averages, as future work. This is an **author research direction**, not a conjectured numerical optimum. Source: *Is Level-1 Blob Reconstruction Under the Network Multispecies Coalescent Easy?*, WABI 2026, DOI [10.4230/LIPIcs.WABI.2026.1](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.WABI.2026.1), **§5 final paragraph, printed p. 1:16**. [Official paper and PDF](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.WABI.2026.1). Full relevant theoretical sections and conclusion inspected.

**Prior art and present boundary.** The same paper already supplies NetCS: level-1 blob reconstruction, given the tree of blobs, with O(n log n) queried four-taxon sets in fast mode and model-dependent correctness/consistency guarantees (§3, especially Corollary 1). Its noisy implementation performs additional computation. Claiming that sparse level-1 reconstruction is new would duplicate this work. Frohn et al. 2025, DOI [10.1016/j.jcss.2025.103655](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcss.2025.103655), gives a closely related quarnet-query baseline; its exact results must be read before a new query-complexity claim.

Our existing [all-level package](https://github.com/Sodelin/Work-on-Samuel-Alexander-Research-/blob/main/research/nanuq-all-level-2026-09-29/ALL-LEVEL-PROOF.md) already gives conditional support recovery using n(n−3)/2 boundary-quartet membership queries when a correct circular order and an **exact displayed-quartet oracle** are provided. The extension ledger contains an anchor-coverage criterion. These are reusable dependencies, not pending discoveries. An NMSC gene quartet can occur through incomplete lineage sorting without being a network-displayed quartet; an empirical frequency table is therefore not that oracle.

**Our narrowed target.** Fix the outer-labeled planar galled class, one declared score family, a supplied correct circular order, and a specified recoverable split target. Determine whether a strictly smaller, selectable/adaptive anchor-query set preserves every required positive split contribution; derive a bound in n and the structural parameter actually needed. Then, separately, construct or reject a finite-sample classifier from NMSC quartet concordance factors into the required oracle answers under explicit parameter separation. Return an exact certificate of selected coverage or a counterexample. This targets **query reduction and a justified data bridge**, not another circular-decomposability proof.

**Missing assumptions/mechanisms.** A query-selection policy; what is known before querying; whether selected queries share the same independent genes; missing taxa and gene-tree estimation error; a biological-model separation margin; a circular-order estimator if end-to-end inference is later claimed. Shared quartet estimates need not be independent; any concentration argument must reflect that.

**Significance and omnibus interface.** Count distinct queried four-taxon sets, runtime/storage, and support error under a declared sampling model. The transferable interface is a certificate mapping **limited observations → recoverable structural properties**, with the uncertain oracle layer explicit.

**Lowest-cost next step.** Read NetCS and the existing anchor-hitset criterion together; on the already enumerated small cases, compare minimum covering query sets with the proposed construction and seek a missing-split counterexample. If the target merely restates NetCS or existing hitset equivalence, record it as prior art and stop before formalization. Search status checked 2026-09-30; no exhaustive later-literature search was performed.

## BIO-2 — From level-2 identifiability to a complete noisy-data algorithm

**Source question.** Holtgrefe et al. state that their constructive proof lays the foundation for a statistically consistent level-2 inference algorithm, with its implementation and performance analysis left to future work. This is an **explicit algorithmic research direction**. Source: *Distinguishing Phylogenetic Level-2 Networks with Quartets and Inter-Taxon Quartet Distances*, DOI [10.1007/s11538-025-01549-4](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11538-025-01549-4), **Discussion**, paragraphs describing the pipeline after Theorem 5.5. [PMC full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12549781/). Theorem 5.5, Proposition 5.4, Discussion, and the listed correction were inspected in the accessible main text; correction contents were not independently audited.

**Prior art and present boundary.** The paper already proves canonical-form identifiability for its class and sketches TINNiK → quartet-derived distance → Neighbor-Net → canonical form. It is incorrect to say that no algorithmic approach exists. Its separate level-3 circularity and parametric-distance questions overlap the omnibus's all-level research package; that existing work must be cited and audited, not solved again. NetCS addresses level-1 reconstruction, not this complete level-2 noisy pipeline. Quarnet/trinet reconstruction algorithms use different input semantics from NMSC summary statistics.

**Our narrowed target.** On the source's outer-labeled planar, galled, level-2 subclass, specify one canonical-form reconstruction algorithm from a finite family of correctly classified quartet relationships. Prove that its output is the identifiable canonical form, with an explicit deterministic robustness condition on the quartet classifier and a finite-sample failure bound under separated NMSC parameters. Scope is a **canonical form**, including the source's indistinguishability contractions, not complete recovery of every reticulation and numerical parameter.

**Missing assumptions/mechanisms.** Exact class/canonical-form definitions; whether the tree of blobs is given or estimated; generic vs uniform parameter assumptions; what happens near unresolved/degenerate quartets; algorithm for fitting/validating circular order; canonical extraction with ties and errors. The current all-level distance certificate does not supply these mechanisms.

**Significance and omnibus interface.** A verified inference composition in which each module exports an explicit success condition and error event. Measure canonical topology error, abstention at non-identifiable cases, runtime and failure probability. This is a reusable template for composing imperfect biological observations into an identifiable output.

**Lowest-cost next step.** Check author follow-up software/publications and the 2026 tree-of-blobs literature before allocating implementation work; reconstruct one existing six-taxon canonical example from the paper's own Table 1, preserving equivalent-network classes. If an already published algorithm covers the same contract, reuse it and only screen genuinely missing robustness/composition pieces. Current-status search 2026-09-30 found nearby new phylogenetic results; it did not certify continued openness of this exact pipeline target.

## BIO-3 — Finite population effects on epigenetic reproductive isolation

**Source direction.** Planidin et al. propose that finite populations **may** magnify the relative strength of epigenetic barriers. This is a **model-extension direction**, not a universal monotonicity conjecture. Source: *Adaptive epigenetic divergence can facilitate ecological speciation*, DOI [10.1098/rspb.2025.1217](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.2025.1217), **§5 Conclusion**. [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40957570/), [PMC main text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12440623/).

**Access and provenance.** PubMed abstract/metadata and the first half of primary main text were freshly inspected. Fresh full-text continuation was blocked by a transient connector error/PMC challenge. The §5 direction and relevant supplement reading are preserved in the [immutable 2026-09-25 source audit](https://github.com/Sodelin/Work-on-Samuel-Alexander-Research-/blob/e2502c82ab9a77c00543932f775a71e5374221f7/research/feedback-speciation/SOURCE-AUDIT.md): main-text coverage, exact life-cycle recursions in supplement pp. 3–5, spectral passages pp. 33–38, and official supplement/code links. This retained audit is the evidence for the exact concluding direction; its passage still needs fresh primary confirmation before submission.

**Prior art and present boundary.** The published baseline is deterministic, two-deme, diploid and two-locus, with a specific migration/selection/recombination/epimutation order and a neutral migrant marker. Its haploid constant-transition spectral solution is prior art. The existing affinity-model fixed-point and exponential-fixation results concern a different gene-culture recurrence; they do not solve this stochastic RI comparison. Finite gene-culture work already exists: Fogarty and Otto 2024, DOI [10.1073/pnas.2322885121](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2322885121), identified in the retained audit; primary technical comparison remains required. A 2026 related empirical paper, DOI [10.1098/rstb.2025.0024](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2025.0024), studies methylation/signalling associations and calls for causal validation; it does not, from its inspected abstract, settle this finite two-deme mathematical comparison.

**Our narrowed target.** Define one exact finite two-deme Markov chain matching the published life cycle, including neutral marker transmission, then compare an epigenetic model with a parameter-matched genetic model. For fixed census N and horizon H, estimate or calculate the difference in expected neutral-marker transmission; alternatively choose eventual fixation probability, but do not equate the two. Find sufficient parameter conditions for the epigenetic barrier to be stronger, or produce an exact small-N counterexample to a proposed universal inequality. Any later N-asymptotic claim must be explicit.

**Missing assumptions/mechanisms.** Diploid parent and migration sampling, recombination, epimutation timing, census/selection normalization, genetic comparator, initialization, and the finite definition of RI. Changing them changes the scientific question. Conditional-on-survival outcomes differ from unconditional marker transmission.

**Significance and omnibus interface.** Identify a regime in which deterministic conclusions do or do not survive stochastic population size; report exact uncertainty/absorption calculations. The interface links inheritance mechanisms and finite stochastic dynamics to observed divergence. It does not infer species membership or human psychopathology.

**Lowest-cost next step.** Confirm §5 from the primary paper and compare the published recursions with one proposed finite life cycle. Only then enumerate the smallest nontrivial population transition matrix using exact rational arithmetic, before any large simulation or Lean corpus. Triage: retained source direction confirmed; **HOLD for primary continuation and exact stochastic specification**. Bounded subsequent search dated 2026-09-30, not a claim that no later solution exists.

## BIO-4 — Identifiability and transport of cellular perturbation responses

**Source question.** Dibaeinia et al. explicitly ask under which assumptions interventional information in a source context identifies a causal perturbation effect in a target context. They argue that contextual coverage, rather than scale alone, limits virtual-cell generalization. This is an **author-stated research question/position**, with an informal theoretical framework, not a solved general theorem. Source: *Virtual Cells Need Context, Not Just Scale*, DOI [10.64898/2026.02.04.703804](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.02.04.703804), **Theoretical Framework → Connection to Causal Transportability, Definition 3.1 and Proposition 3.2; Context Problem and Why Implicit Context Inference Fails**. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41727131/), [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12919078/). Preprint status as indexed; body inspected through 28,000 of 33,728 characters, including the named theoretical sections. Discussion remainder not inspected.

**Prior art and present boundary.** Causal transportability theory is established prior art explicitly cited by the source; generic impossibility from changing mechanisms is not our discovery. Recent partial progress includes scPILOT, DOI [10.1002/advs.77461](https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.77461), online 2026-08-29 ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42667121/)); its inspected abstract transfers **previously observed perturbations** across contexts and reports query-context dependence. It does not justify arbitrary unseen mechanism transfer. CausCell and scCausalVI also address causal cellular representations under their own assumptions; they must be compared at a selected exact estimand.

**Our narrowed target.** Specify a finite class of context-indexed structural causal models and a fixed perturbation-response query q. Given observational target data and a declared source intervention table, characterize the possible q-values over all compatible models. Determine the smallest additional context/perturbation measurements that make this query set a singleton, or reduce its width below a declared tolerance. Test whether a proposed compressed cellular observation is sufficient for q across held-out contexts. This formalizes **prediction identification without requiring full mechanism reconstruction**. A generic version-space singleton observation is already elementary prior work; the contribution would have to be the biologically justified class, sharp measurement requirements, or an efficient selection method.

**Missing assumptions/mechanisms.** Which causal mechanisms are stable, which vary by tissue/donor/time, overlap of perturbations across contexts, measurement model, latent chromatin/protein/metabolic variables, randomized assignment, and selection effects. Similar RNA profiles need not imply identical responses. Context labels are not automatically sufficient statistics.

**Significance and omnibus interface.** Report intervention/context coverage, identified response intervals, and calibrated held-out errors. The shared omnibus interface is **data-compatible model set V(D) → query response set R_q(D)**, with explicit transport assumptions. The same mathematics can later support psychological and social response queries without claiming that their mechanisms are the same.

**Lowest-cost next step.** Compare one finite query-specific transport contract with the source-cited transportability results and scPILOT's actual training/test partitions. Use a tiny constructed observationally equivalent pair to test the claimed information requirement before training a model. **CANDIDATE**, not a declared novel theorem. Search status 2026-09-30 includes subsequent methods and prospective critiques; general cellular transport remains an active agenda, but exact-target novelty is unresolved.

## BIO-5 — Directed cell interactions beyond observational equivalence

**Source direction.** Megas et al. say Celcomen's ability could be improved by supervised training on spatial transcriptomic perturbation data. Observational information alone cannot, without further assumptions, fully orient the causal graph. This is an **author extension direction**. Source: *Celcomen: spatial causal disentanglement for single-cell and tissue perturbation modeling*, DOI [10.1038/s41467-026-69856-5](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-69856-5), **Discussion**, third paragraph; **Methods → Model: Theoretical foundations**, assumptions and Proposition 2. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41851134/), [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13149852/). Discussion and theoretical Methods were inspected through 44,000 of 47,133 body characters; supplementary proof not read.

**Prior art and present boundary.** The paper already supplies its own parametric identifiability result. It distinguishes the learned undirected structure from complete directed causal identification, assumes causal sufficiency, and requires single-cell resolution to separate intracellular from intercellular terms; multi-cell spots change the estimand to intra-/inter-spot regulation. Merely Lean-transcribing Proposition 2 is supporting verification, not an open-problem solution. Intervention-based orientation and Markov equivalence are established theory.

**Our narrowed target.** Choose a finite linear acyclic two-cell model with known spatial adjacency and explicitly separated intra- and inter-cell blocks. For one directed response query q, characterize the admissible causal orientations/effects compatible with observational covariance and a limited mask of single-gene interventions. Establish which intervention masks identify q even when the whole directed graph remains unidentified; test whether aggregating cells into spots destroys that identification. Start with exact rank/separation conditions and explicit countermodels. The selected class and novelty must first be checked against existing intervention-design results.

**Missing assumptions/mechanisms.** Faithfulness, acyclicity vs biological feedback, intervention precision, latent confounding, noise covariance, measurement mixing, cell resolution, and whether conditional clamping implements the intended do-intervention. In particular, generative conditioning and causal intervention are different operations unless justified by the model.

**Significance and omnibus interface.** Measure the number/type of perturbations needed for a defined response, the width of remaining effect sets, and losses from sensor aggregation. This connects molecular coupling to tissue-scale observation without mistaking identifiable model parameters for a uniquely confirmed biological mechanism.

**Lowest-cost next step.** Read the supplemental identifiability proof and cited causal-orientation results; list exactly what its Proposition 2 identifies. Reject a target already covered by those results. If a missing aggregation/intervention-mask question survives, enumerate a two-cell counterexample first. Triage **CANDIDATE**; no general clinical/tissue counterfactual validity asserted.

## Compute and discovery guard

The initial research queue should contain **one** admitted target, with BIO-1 the strongest reuse candidate. BIO-3 is a second feasible formal target after its life cycle is fixed. BIO-4–5 need domain/class selection before proof work. A short empirical gate is warranted across virtual-cell entries: the Sept. 2026 Perspective [Explicit Mechanistic Operators in Predictive Virtual Cells](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42782810/) (DOI [10.3390/cells15181711](https://doi.org/10.3390/cells15181711), **§1.3 Central Question**) asks whether specified biological operators improve held-out prediction beyond capacity/data/compute-matched alternatives. Its framework is prospective. That is a reason to test incremental value before building a large mechanistic corpus, not evidence that formalization itself improves prediction.

Supporting lemmas, exact representation checks, and source recursions are reused where they are dependencies. Formalizing all available biology is not the admission criterion. Every proposed new target must survive a closest-result comparison, show a consequential unresolved gap, and identify the smallest observation/proof experiment that distinguishes progress from duplicate prior art.
