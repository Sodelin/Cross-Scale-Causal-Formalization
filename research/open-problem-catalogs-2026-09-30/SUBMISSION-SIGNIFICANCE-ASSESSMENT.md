Research date: **2026-09-30**. This is a bounded venue check and prospective contribution assessment. None of the four proposed targets is a newly proved result, a submission-ready packet, or certified novel here.

## What the actual venue evaluates

The retained [NANUQ delivery receipt](https://github.com/Sodelin/Work-on-Samuel-Alexander-Research-/blob/main/research/nanuq-all-level-2026-09-29/DELIVERY-RECEIPT.json) identifies the venue as **VibeMathed**, https://vibemathed.com. Its captured state is submitted under review, not acceptance; the receipt does not establish current queue status.

The official [methodology](https://vibemathed.com/methodology), sections **What belongs here**, **Result and status**, **The verification ladder**, and **Significance**, was directly retrieved. It requires a previously stated open question answered by a theorem or disproof, with substantive AI involvement. Merely formalizing a result humans already proved, empirical findings, and heuristic improvements are outside scope. New bounds or resolved special cases can qualify as partial results; a neighboring reinterpretation is a variant. Lean checks the supplied statement; independent correspondence checking is needed for the strongest formal label.

The significance score measures **the problem's standing before its resolution**, not the presentation's polish or the quantity of formalization. It is assigned by curators using comparative anchors. The published anchors include community-famous conjectures around 30, typical numbered Erdős problems around 10, and machine-generated conjectures around 5. These are calibration examples, not predicted scores for our projects. The [curator checklist](https://github.com/mrconter1/vibemathed/blob/main/docs/reviewing.md), §§2, 5–8, separates scope, verification, resolution, and significance. The [contribution guide](https://vibemathed.com/contributing), **What a good submission has**, asks for a public primary source, substantive model disclosure, and honest status.

**My judgment:** the present leads have specialist or emerging methodological significance. There is presently no evidence for landmark standing. Strong presentation can make their actual advance legible and prevent scope confusion; it cannot promote an obscure restricted question into a famous one. A high-impact scientific agenda also does not automatically confer that standing on a small theorem extracted from it.

## The four contribution frontiers

| Target | Small useful advance | Stronger advance worth pursuing | Best honest prospective pitch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **BIO-1: sparse quartet inference** | A genuinely new coverage construction or lower bound for the declared network class and oracle; not the existing anchor-hit equivalence | An improved query bound with a justified noisy NMSC-data interface and a matching limitation, compared with NetCS/quarnet baselines | “We reduce the observations needed to recover specified evolutionary-network structure, and prove when biological sampling supports the required decisions.” |

| **Shared noisy decision-sufficient representations** | A new special-case noisy guarantee that answers the source's stated extension; not rederived sufficiency or ordinary concentration | Matching measurement/sample bounds under specified noise, geometry, and costs, resolving the repeated-measurement versus independent-direction obstruction | “We establish the information cost of learning measurements that preserve optimization decisions under noise.” |

These are **proposed deltas**, not results claimed in hand. Strong means the advance changes what can be inferred, measured, or optimized across a substantive class, rather than adds a convenient lemma.

## Why the current corpus does not already supply them

**BIO-1** is anchored in Dai and Molloy, [WABI 2026](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.WABI.2026.1), §5 final paragraph, printed p.1:16. NetCS already offers sparse level-1 reconstruction. Our all-level package has exact structural oracle conditions; observed gene-quartet frequencies are not automatically that oracle. A better hit-set argument alone cannot claim an end-to-end biological gain.

**PSY-1** comes from Robinaugh et al., [2020 network-psychopathology agenda](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0033291719003404), **Network Psychometrics → Critical Analysis & Future Directions**. This is a methodological direction, not a verbatim mathematical conjecture. Our additive measurement bounds and abstraction modules omit the clinical dynamics and intervention semantics. The catalog names a 2024 in-silico intervention method that must be compared. Venue fit is weakest here until a precise externally posed mathematical subquestion is established; clinical importance remains a separate assessment.

**SOC-03** comes from Volchenkov, [2026 author preprint](https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202608.0726), §3.4; the [published version](https://doi.org/10.3390/math14183363) was inaccessible beyond metadata during the catalog check. The index and quantifier over balanced outcomes remain unfixed. Balanced-outcome algorithms are prior art. Our two-person budget theorem contains none of the required graph/index objects. This is the cleanest direct ranking-correspondence lead once those definitions and closest class theorems are settled.

**The shared direction** is explicitly mathematical in Ye, Amin and Özdağlar, [COLT 2026](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v336/ye26a.html), final §7 pp.12–13. The omnibus's [existing attempt](../noisy-shadow-open-question-attempt-2026-09-30.md) already reports a failed confidence-tube argument: repeated noisy measurements need not add independent directions. Its iid-corruption corollary is supporting prior-art reasoning. A later result must repair the substantive obstruction. This source was not independently reread in this bounded venue pass.

## What carries across the omnibus

The shared contribution is a **query-specific distinction**: which observationally compatible structures still give different answers, and which additional information removes that disagreement? Biological split recovery, psychological intervention ranking, and social payoff ordering instantiate different answers and mechanisms. Their source models, assumptions, and scientific validation stay in their individual projects. A common interface and reusable mathematical dependency can connect them; a successful theorem in one domain does not close another domain's open question.

No outreach, new submission, proof build, or acceptance prediction was performed. Before proposing a venue score, compare the exact finished problem with named published entries of similar standing; before submitting, establish original-question correspondence and a genuinely new theorem.
