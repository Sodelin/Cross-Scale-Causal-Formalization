# Bio–psycho–social open-problem catalogs

Recorded **30 September 2026 UTC** for Nolan Downard's Cross-Scale-Causal-Formalization omnibus. The omnibus is a distributed research program: individual projects answer externally motivated questions; shared interfaces let their contributions connect without conflating their scientific meanings.

This package contains **14 source-backed questions or research directions: five biological, four psychological, and five social**. It is a catalog for selecting investigations, not fourteen certified novel theorem targets. Author-posed questions, model-extension directions, and our narrowed proposals have different status. No new proof, empirical result, or open-problem closure is claimed.

The choices above prioritize reusable leverage and a cheap decisive comparison. They do not authorize a large proof program. BIO-4 is the strongest general cross-scale interface candidate; PSY-3 is a second compact psychological inference probe; SOC-01 offers a biology/social bridge but already has substantial classifier prior art.

## What each entry supplies

The domain files record a primary source and exact locator, what it asks, closest known progress, the separate proposed target, missing assumptions, meaningful output, omnibus interface and smallest next discriminator. Companion JSON files preserve stable IDs and search/access records. [INDEX.json](INDEX.json) is a compact cross-catalog index, not a second authority that replaces those records.

Candidate inclusion means **worth investigating**. A source calling something open does not prove our precise formulation remains unsolved today. HOLD records specify what comparison or model decision is missing. Bounded search coverage is explicit; absent hits do not establish novelty. There is no requirement that a valuable result have a guaranteed adopter.

This is targeted recovery across relevant repositories, **not an assertion that every file, branch, issue or private project was exhaustively read**.

The existing all-level NANUQ support/circularity work is a prior result with retained model restrictions. Repeating level-three or all-level circularity is not a new queue item. Its structural oracle does not automatically become an inference guarantee for biological gene-tree frequencies. Likewise, elementary measurement intervals, exact quotients and connectivity lemmas are useful dependencies; they do not by themselves answer the named clinical or social questions.

## How this applies the theory

[OMNIBUS-MAP.md](OMNIBUS-MAP.md) separates the hidden system, observation, compatible-model set, requested response and cross-context/scale map. It asks which restrictions the mechanism genuinely needs, and which further observation would change a consequential inference. This supplies target discovery rather than merely checking that smaller tasks were completed.

Shared abstractions are referenced as prior work. We are not constructing a new generic theorem corpus merely because the vocabulary connects. Each scientific bridge still requires its own source correspondence, measurement law and preserved response.
