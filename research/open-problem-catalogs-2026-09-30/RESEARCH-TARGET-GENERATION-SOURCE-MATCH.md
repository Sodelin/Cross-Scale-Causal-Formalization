# Research-target generation: exact source match and useful frontier

Research date: **2026-09-30**. Bounded primary-source check; no experiment, new proof, or submission launched.

## The answer to Nolan's question

**The broad problem has been posed. Our proposed matched-budget test is not currently an exact solution to a previously stated mathematical open problem.** It tests Nolan's operational hypothesis, whose methodological novelty remains unverified: explicitly searching for outcome-relevant missing distinctions and stronger source-matched targets may produce more useful research than ordinary task completion or generic checking. That is a potentially useful empirical contribution, but it is not automatically a new theorem or open-problem closure.

Three tasks must remain distinct: **forming a research target**, **strengthening a theorem already selected**, and **eliciting observations/assumptions needed to answer a target**. The **all levels incident** (the NANUQ episode) concerned the second. An ablation measuring better questions concerns the first. Cinelli's most direct source challenge concerns the third. One successful trial cannot claim to solve all three.

## The useful empirical response

The maximal meaningful empirical claim is **a reproducible improvement in consequential research output per total resource cost across a declared distribution of tasks**, together with failure boundaries. Total cost includes literature screening, coordination, attempted proofs, verification, and discarded candidates. Output should distinguish source fidelity, genuinely new result, useful scope, and verification. Counting completed subtasks, words, or unanimous self-audits reproduces the failure Nolan described.

A matched-budget trial is an intermediate discriminator for that claim. It should compare the same tasks, sources, model access, and costs; independently assess results; retain failed attempts; and distinguish stronger statements from merely larger statements. Its practical utility is choosing where to spend research effort. It cannot establish the model's internal reward mechanism or universal optimality. Those are separate hypotheses.

## A maximal useful mathematical target within a class

The closest defensible mathematical route is an **interactive causal-knowledge elicitation frontier**, motivated by Cinelli rather than presented as his verbatim conjecture:

Given a declared finite or structured causal-model class, initial data-compatible models, a target effect q, admissible knowledge/measurement questions, their response semantics and costs, return the sharp possible q-values after each exact, truthful answer. For noisy answers, use a specified response likelihood or calibrated confidence rule; report the resulting confidence-qualified set rather than treating expert answers as certain model exclusions. Characterize when q can be identified, the minimum worst-case cost of identifying it, and the strongest answer achievable at each budget. Supply an algorithm with a matching lower bound or an explicitly bounded approximation guarantee. Permit abstention when the available question family cannot separate outcome-relevant alternatives.

That is the **fixed-representation baseline**. To capture Nolan's missing-search-space concern, a discovery-aware extension must also specify generation/refinement operations, account for their cost, preserve the intended response under representation changes, and justify coverage of outcome-relevant distinctions. A bounded witness may exist without the generator ever finding it. Proposing a new distinction alone neither supplies evidence nor removes a compatible model. Without a justified coverage condition, sound stopping cannot certify that undiscovered distinctions do not matter.

That contract exposes the whole intended frontier before proof work: class size/structure, adaptive versus fixed queries, noisy versus exact answers, point versus partial identification, computational cost, and cross-context transfer. Removing one incidental level restriction does not silently substitute for the rest. Generality may require restrictions; their necessity should be proved or witnessed where possible.

Necessary intermediate obligations are source-model/answer semantics, preservation of compatible models, sharp query-answer correspondence, characterization of inseparable models, and the domain-specific algorithm/lower bound. Generic version-space elimination, decision-tree recurrences, or Bellman optimality are dependencies to reuse. Their formalization alone is not the scientific advance. The new contribution would need a nontrivial causal class, richer constraints, sharper complexity frontier, or a substantive source-specific guarantee absent from closest work.
