## Which source questions are answered?

Primary source: Holtgrefe et al., *Distinguishing Phylogenetic Level-2 Networks with Quartets and Inter-Taxon Quartet Distances*, [DOI 10.1007/s11538-025-01549-4](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11538-025-01549-4), Theorem 4.7 and Section 6 final paragraphs. The coordinator reopened the current publisher text, including Definitions 2.1–2.4 and the three future directions.

| Published question | Current package | What is not implied |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Extend Theorem 4.7's circularity and exact displayed-split support beyond single-blob level-two networks | Theorem A supplies both conclusions for arbitrary blob counts and every finite level within the source structural class | Unique reconstruction of every hidden network, or statistical recovery from biological samples |
| Prove level-three bloblet circularity | Included in the stronger all-finite-level Theorem A | Circularity for arbitrary nonplanar or nongalled networks |
| Extend some results to a parametric distance family | Theorem B gives an affirmative extension: an exact universal anchor-positivity region and sufficient all-level bloblet circularity | Maximal unweighted-distance region, global multi-blob parameter composition, or exact support throughout that region |

The exact Theorem A class is **finite binary semi-directed LSA-rootable, outer-labeled planar, galled networks with at least four taxa**. It uses uniform means of **distinct displayed quartet topologies**, not switching-frequency weights. The conclusion is circular decomposability of the original NANUQ distance and equality of its positive split support with the union of displayed-tree splits.

Canonical evidence is in the [Samuel theorem package](https://github.com/Sodelin/Work-on-Samuel-Alexander-Research-/blob/e2502c82ab9a77c00543932f775a71e5374221f7/research/nanuq-all-level-2026-09-29/README.md), [proof](https://github.com/Sodelin/Work-on-Samuel-Alexander-Research-/blob/e2502c82ab9a77c00543932f775a71e5374221f7/research/nanuq-all-level-2026-09-29/ALL-LEVEL-PROOF.md), and [source correspondence](https://github.com/Sodelin/Work-on-Samuel-Alexander-Research-/blob/e2502c82ab9a77c00543932f775a71e5374221f7/research/nanuq-all-level-2026-09-29/SOURCE-RESULT-LEDGER.md). ## The 85-declaration soft-science baseline

The count is current: **16 solidarity + 51 foundations + 18 clinical = 85 theorem declarations**. They include supporting lemmas, endpoints, witnesses, consequences, integrity checks and explicit reuse. They are not 85 independent discoveries or externally posed open-question resolutions. See the [baseline assessment](85-BASELINE.md) for the complete count and model-to-target map.

The corpus has direct useful connections to the omnibus: observation equivalence and information loss, bounded measurement bias, causal ambiguity, evidence-compatible hypotheses, collective-action constraints, aggregation examples, and a pinned CBT transition/observation fragment.

Two precise limitations matter for Nolan's theory:

1. Current `Identified` asks whether the **entire parameter** is recoverable. Many target questions only need a response q or ranking to agree across compatible models. Reuse the equivalence machinery; do not demand unnecessary full recovery.
2. `distinguishing_query_eliminates_rival` assumes the distinguishing input and correct label have been supplied. It does not discover the question, establish noisy-answer reliability or minimize its cost. Target discovery remains missing even when the conditional update theorem is proved.

These observations identify interfaces to implement when a substantive project needs them, not a reason to create a new generic theorem corpus immediately. The CBT fragment likewise omits full belief inference, policy selection, stochastic learning and the proposed partial-approach extension. Its checked observation contrast does not establish clinical efficacy.
