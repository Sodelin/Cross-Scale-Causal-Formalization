# Portable strongest-target handoff

Prepared 30 September 2026 UTC. This routes existing work; it does not claim live chat-to-chat messaging or launch a proof attempt.

## The baseline lesson

Before executing a convenient special case, identify the strongest useful source-matched statement and where each restriction enters its mechanism. If a restriction is unused, test the broader statement immediately. If it is needed, produce the obstruction. Correctness checking of a selected target cannot by itself discover an excluded better target. Do not turn this into a requirement to generate paperwork or extra lemmas.
